Our story

From a bicycle cart to the harbour's living room

We wanted somewhere you could get properly good coffee, an honest plate of food and a seat by the water, without any fuss. So we built it.

How it started

In 2019 Naledi was roasting coffee in her garage and Sam was cooking breakfasts for friends every weekend. They bolted an espresso machine to a cargo bicycle, parked it on the quay at 6am and sold out by nine.

The fishermen came back the next day. So did the joggers, the dog walkers and the harbour pilots. Two years later, with a waiting list for the cart's cardamom buns, they signed the lease on an old ship chandler's store on Wharf Lane.

Today the store is a 60-seat café with a deck over the water, and the garage roaster has grown into a small roastery two streets away.

  1. 2019

    The coffee cart opens on the quay at sunrise.

  2. 2021

    We move into 21 Wharf Lane and start serving food.

  3. 2023

    Our own roastery opens, two streets from the café.

  4. 2025

    The deck over the water opens, dogs very much included.

What we care about

Three things we won't compromise on

We roast it ourselves

We buy green beans directly from three farms we know by name and roast them in small batches every week.

We cook from scratch

Bread, curries, sambals, sauces and every bake. If we can make it in our kitchen, we do.

We look after the harbour

Compostable cups, a refill discount and a monthly beach clean-up that anyone is welcome to join.
