Our story

It started with a coffee cart on the quay

Naledi and Sam sold their first flat whites to fishermen and early-morning joggers from a converted bicycle cart in 2019.

Five years later there's a café, a small roastery and a kitchen that still makes everything by hand: bread, sauces, sambals and the cardamom buns people queue for on Saturdays.
